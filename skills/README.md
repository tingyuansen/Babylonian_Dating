# Skills

`dating_protocol.md` is the written protocol of the dating rules, for agents and human auditors who check a record (SI Appendix, section S2). It sets the three possible decisions, committed, ranked or decline. It also gives the rules for reading the text, for the tiers of evidence and for scoring the candidate years.

The results record the SHA-256 of this file, so it stays exactly as it was when the results were made.
